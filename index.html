📖 View Full Academic Abstract & Mathematical Formulations ↓

Abstract: Decentralized renewable energy penetration poses stability challenges to synchronous utility grids due to intermittency. This project, conceived and engineered by Yogesh Chendel, bridges academic theory with practical application by delivering a unified digital portal featuring:

  1. Photovoltaic Yield Equation: E = A × r × H × PR (where A = array area, r = panel efficiency, H = annual average solar radiation on tilted panels, and PR = Performance Ratio ≈ 0.75 – 0.80).
  2. Wind Power Aerodynamic Extraction: P = 0.5 × ρ × A × v³ × Cp, bounded by the Betz limit (Cp,max ≈ 59.3%).
  3. Battery Energy Storage Dispatch: Real-time load-following algorithm calculating charge state (SoCt+1 = SoCt + ηch · Pexcess · Δt) during peak solar hours and discharge replenishment during twilight demand peaks.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Most tier-1 mono-crystalline silicon panels feature performance warranties of 25 to 30 years. Modern panels degrade at less than 0.5% per year, meaning they still operate at over 85% of their original capacity after 25 years.

On-Grid systems connect to your local utility provider with a bi-directional net meter, feeding surplus solar power into the grid without requiring expensive batteries. Off-Grid systems rely on battery energy storage banks to operate completely independent of the utility grid.

Modern utility wind turbines utilize variable pitch control to maximize aerodynamic lift across varying wind speeds (cut-in around 3 m/s). Hybrid microgrids pair wind with solar and pumped storage hydro so that nocturnal winds compensate for nighttime solar downtime.

We apply standard emission factors from the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) and International Energy Agency (IEA), where each 1 kWh generated from coal thermal power generates roughly 0.82 kg of CO₂ equivalent. Replacing this with solar or wind offsets this exact burden.
